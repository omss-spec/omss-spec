# OMSS Implementation Registry

This document contains a public registry of implementations, libraries, and tooling built around the Open Media Streaming Specification (OMSS).

The registry is community-maintained and accepts submissions through [registry entry requests](https://github.com/omss-spec/omss-spec/issues/new?template=registry_entry_request.yml). **Entries may be added, updated, or removed at the discretion of maintainers and administrators.**

Implementations listed here may be open source or closed source, provided they follow the OMSS specification.

**Inclusion in this registry does not imply certification, endorsement, compliance guarantees, security review, or quality assurance by the OMSS project.**

## Submission Requirements

To request a registry change, [open a Registry Entry Request](https://github.com/omss-spec/omss-spec/issues/new?template=registry_entry_request.yml).
